The ACE Framework

A Methodology Built Around Root Cause.

The ACE Framework connects three questions: how does your business operate, where are the compliance gaps, and are your processes producing results?

A

Audit

Map how your business actually operates — not how you think it does. We surface the gap between stated process and real behavior using structured analysis tailored to your industry.

C

Compliance

Identify gaps in OSHA, data privacy, HR documentation, HIPAA, and more — before a regulator does. Every gap is traced to its root cause, not just flagged on a checklist.

E

Efficacy

Measure whether your tools, SOPs, and processes are producing results. Identify silent failures before they become visible ones. Set a baseline. Prove improvement.

Audit — What We Look At

  • Workflow mapping vs. how work actually flows
  • Handoff gaps and communication breakdowns
  • Documentation accuracy and version control
  • Vendor and contractor process alignment
  • Technology stack utilization and redundancy
  • Bottlenecks causing rework or delays

Compliance — What We Scan For

  • OSHA safety standards and documentation gaps
  • HIPAA / healthcare privacy exposure points
  • HR documentation: I-9s, classifications, policies
  • DOL wage, hour, and timekeeping compliance
  • Data privacy obligations (state and federal)
  • Contractor vs. employee misclassification risk

Efficacy — What We Measure

  • SOP adherence and real-world execution gaps
  • Tool ROI: are your systems actually used?
  • Process cycle time and throughput baselines
  • Rework rates and hidden cost of errors
  • KPI alignment: what's tracked vs. what matters
  • Remediation results and improvement verification
