The ACE Framework

A Methodology Built Around Root Cause.

Most consultants arrive with a checklist. We arrive with a diagnostic engine. The ACE Framework — Audit, Compliance, Efficacy — is how we find what's broken, why it's broken, and what it will take to fix it permanently.

A

Audit

Map how your business actually operates — not how you think it does. AI tools surface the gap between stated process and real behavior.

C

Compliance

Identify gaps in OSHA, data privacy, HR documentation, HIPAA, and more. We trace each gap to its root cause — not just flag it.

E

Efficacy

Measure whether your tools, SOPs, and processes are actually working. Set baselines. Identify silent failures. Prove improvement.

Built on Operational Discipline.

Scout Consultatory was founded in 2020 by Brandon Worobi — Army veteran, logistics operator, and IT professional. The name is intentional: you scout before you act. You understand the terrain before you commit resources.

That philosophy drives every engagement. We audit before we recommend. We trace root causes before we propose fixes. We use AI and technology as diagnostic tools — not buzzwords — because they genuinely improve the accuracy and speed of operational analysis.

Scout works best with small and mid-sized businesses that have outgrown their current systems but haven't yet built the infrastructure to scale cleanly. If your operations are running on instinct, workarounds, and tribal knowledge — that's where we work best.
