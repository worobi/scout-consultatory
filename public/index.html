About Scout Consultatory

Scout Consultatory was founded in 2020 by Brandon Worobi — Army veteran, logistics operator, IT professional, and someone who's spent time in industries ranging from behavioral health to enterprise tech.

That background matters. It means we understand operations at the ground level, not just from a boardroom. We've worked in the mess. We know what "disorganized" actually looks like and how to fix it without blowing everything up.

Our edge isn't tools — it's seeing the full picture: the broken workflow, the communication gap, the manual process that's eating 10 hours a week. We fix those things end-to-end.
