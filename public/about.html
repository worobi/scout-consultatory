The Story

You Scout Before You Act.

Scout Consultatory was founded by Brandon Worobi — Army veteran, logistics operator, and operations and IT professional with years of hands-on experience across multiple industries. Brandon holds certifications and formal education in operations management, IT systems, and compliance frameworks, and brings that background directly to every client engagement.

The approach is straightforward: you scout before you act. Understand the terrain, find the root cause, then fix it right the first time. Every recommendation is grounded in documented findings — not gut feel, and not generic best practices that don't fit your business.

Scout works across all applicable industries — healthcare, logistics, construction, retail, professional services, property management, and more. The methodology adapts to what your industry requires. The tools used depend on what the situation calls for. Technology is one of them — not the only one.

Scout Consultatory is licensed, insured and bonded, carrying Errors & Omissions and General Liability coverage up to $1,000,000 — so you can engage with confidence.
